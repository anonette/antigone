Change “John” to “you” at the trolley switch and human permissibility falls from about 90 to 65 percent (Nadelhoffer and Feltz 2008). Change only the language of the burial dilemma and five of six models change their verdict (this study). A benchmark fixes one pronoun and one language for every item, so its score describes the model in one voice and reports it as the model.
Speaker position is the grammatical person of the test item as presented to the model. “Translated from English” keeps English person marking, explicit agents and hedging habits under a different surface language. Item formats for MoralChoice, MoralExceptQA, Social Chemistry, Moral Machine, MultiTP, XSafety and the multilingual DIT checked against the papers (Sept 2026); the rest follow the published example items.
